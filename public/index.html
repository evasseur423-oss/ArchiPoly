ArchiPoly

Unified & Open-Source Architectural Software Suite

🌟 5 Workspaces Architecture

  • [01. Material Library]: Categorized PBR catalog & viewer with automatic texture normalization (_baseColor, _normal, _roughness, etc.) and 4 architectural 3D preview meshes.
  • [02. 3D Inspector]: High-performance universal 3D viewer (GLTF, OBJ, FBX, STL, DAE, PLY) with checkerboard UV inspection, 2D UV unwrapping view, layer manager, and comprehensive mesh metrics.
  • [03. PBR Alchemist]: Seamless photo-to-PBR generator (high-pass delighter + Sobel normal filters) mathematically deriving 5 PBR texture maps with real-time 3D preview.
  • [04. Scale Slicer]: Geometric plane-mesh slicing for laser cutting and physical architectural scale model fabrication (1:50 to 1:1000 scales, SVG & DXF vector exports).
  • [05. ArchPhysics]: Thermal engineering lab (ISO 6946 / ISO 13790 with 3D Heatmap) and structural mechanics / mechanics of materials (Euler-Bernoulli with exportable calculation reports).

⚡ Optimizations & Inter-Module Bridges

• Strict GPU Lifecycle: Inactive workspaces automatically suspend their WebGL requestAnimationFrame loop to 0% GPU load on tab switch.
• Alchemist ➔ Library Bridge: Direct saving of generated PBR texture sets into external physical storage.
• 3D Inspector ➔ Slicer / ArchPhysics Bridge: Instant geometry transfer across workspaces without re-importing from disk.
